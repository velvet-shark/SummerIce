# SummerIce privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

SummerIce is a browser extension that summarizes the page you are on with an AI provider you choose. SummerIce has no servers of its own, and the developer never receives your data.

## What SummerIce stores

In your browser's local extension storage:

- the AI provider and model you picked
- your API key for that provider
- your summary preferences (length, format, YouTube caption setting)

In your browser's session storage, which is cleared when you close the browser:

- recent summaries, for up to 24 hours, so reopening one does not call the provider again

This data never leaves your browser except as described below.

## What SummerIce sends, and where

SummerIce only reads a page when you ask for a summary by clicking its icon or pressing its keyboard shortcut. It then:

- extracts the page title and main text, or for a YouTube video, fetches the caption track from YouTube
- sends that text, with your API key, directly from your browser to the provider you chose: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google (Gemini) or xAI (Grok)

The provider's own privacy policy and API terms apply to what you send them.

## What SummerIce does not do

- No analytics, tracking or ads
- No accounts
- No selling or sharing of data
- No reading of pages you have not asked it to summarize
